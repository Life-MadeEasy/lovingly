Questions

Good to know.

What is LOVINGLY?+

LOVINGLY is a premium digital celebration studio. We create personalised invitation and celebration experiences around your story, people and occasion — not a one-size-fits-all template.

What occasions can I create one for?+

Weddings, engagements, birthdays, anniversaries, baby celebrations, housewarmings, milestones and other moments worth gathering people around.

Can I use my own photos and content?+

Absolutely. Your photographs, words, dates, venues and personal details are what make the experience yours.

How do I share my LOVINGLY experience?+

You receive a shareable link that can be sent through WhatsApp, Instagram, email, SMS or wherever your guests already are.

How long does it take?+

Timing depends on the scope and level of customisation. Once we know what you have in mind, we'll give you a clear delivery timeline before we begin.

Can I request a custom design?+

Yes. Custom design is at the heart of LOVINGLY. Tell us the feeling, story and details you want the experience to carry, and we'll shape the design around them.
